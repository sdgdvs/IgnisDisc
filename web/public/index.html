Burn CDs and DVDs, rip music CDs, and back up folders on Windows.

IgnisDisc is a free, open-source disc burner and audio CD ripper for Windows 10 and 11. Burn files, folders, and .iso images to CD or DVD, make audio CDs from WAV, FLAC, MP3, M4A, or WMA tracks, rip your music CDs to lossless audio with automatic track names, or split a large folder across several numbered backup discs. After a burn it can read the whole disc back and compare it with what you asked for. Burning goes through Windows' built-in disc burning service by default; for CDs you can switch on IgnisDisc's own drive commands instead (experimental), which also write CD-Text and always check the disc afterwards. No extra drivers, no ads, no bundled offers, and no tracking.

Technical Overview & Subsystem Architecture

IgnisDisc Optical Studio is a .NET 10 WPF desktop application (IgnisDisc.exe) and a command-line tool (ignisdisc-cli.exe). Its own image builder writes ISO 9660 + Joliet + UDF bridge volumes (UDF 1.02 by default, 1.50 to 2.60 on request), El Torito BIOS/UEFI boot images, and optional SHA256SUMS / BLAKE3SUMS hash lists; legacy disc images (.nrg, .mdf/.mds, .ccd/.img) are read and converted. Discs are burned through Windows IMAPIv2 by default. CDs can instead be written with IgnisDisc's own SCSI MMC commands (experimental, off by default): Session-at-Once audio with CD-Text, and Track-at-Once data. Reading uses Win32 SCSI pass-through: 294-byte C2 error pointers, AccurateRip checksums and read-offset detection for audio, and after a burn a sector-by-sector comparison in which the drive is told to read the disc rather than its buffer. Multi-disc archives carry per-disc SHA-256 manifests and PAR2 recovery volumes. In-memory virtual drives stand in for hardware in the test suite.

CdBurner.Core

  • IsoUdfImageBuilder: ISO 9660, Joliet UCS-2BE, OSTA UDF 1.02 to 2.60 (metadata partition and mirror from 2.50), El Torito BIOS/UEFI boot catalogs; byte-identical output with a fixed build time.
  • Streaming LegacyDiscImageConverter for .nrg, .mdf/.mds, and .ccd/.img (incl. Mode 2 / XA 2336B/2448B sectors).
  • RedBookPcmConverter (polyphase resampler + TPDF dither), built-in FlacFileCodec, CUE/playlist parsers, ArchiveSpanPlanner, and built-in SHA-256 and BLAKE3 hash lists.

CdBurner.Hardware

  • Background ApartmentState.MTA worker (MtaBurnWorker) driving IDiscFormat2Data, IDiscFormat2TrackAtOnce, IDiscFormat2RawCD, and IDiscFormat2Erase.
  • PhysicalSectorReader + Win32ScsiTransport (IOCTL_SCSI_PASS_THROUGH_DIRECT, Full TOC 0x02, READ CD with C2 pointers, READ (10) with FUA for the check after a burn).
  • In-memory virtual drives for tests (VirtualDiscBackend, VirtualMmcDrive) and a compatibility test matrix of 32 simulated drive and media combinations.

CdBurner.Cli & App

  • IgnisDisc.exe: WPF desktop studio with 8 dedicated workspaces and OS-synced Dark/Light theme (ThemeManager).
  • ignisdisc-cli.exe: 17 commands with --json output; it refuses to replace the contents of a rewritable disc unless --overwrite is given.
  • Per-user installer (net48 bootstrapper, no administrator rights) with a small separate Uninstall-IgnisDisc.exe.

Direct drive commands (experimental)

  • Audio CDs: Session-at-Once with SEND CUE SHEET and WRITE (10): CD-Text in the lead-in (R-W sub-channel), ISRC and catalog codes, pre-emphasis and copy bits, INDEX 00 gaps, and audio in front of track 1.
  • Data CDs: one Mode 1 track written Track-at-Once at a speed taken from the drive's own list.
  • Checks: every direct burn is read back and compared (track list, CD-Text, codes, every audio sample or data sector). A laser-off test write is available, and every drive command is logged.
  • Status: off by default; IMAPIv2 stays the default writer and handles DVD and Blu-ray. Run on real hardware on one drive (LG GP60NB50), on CD-R and CD-RW.
